# Third-party software

`gld-patch.sh` downloads and builds the following libraries from their official
sources (checksums are verified). They are copied into the patched app.

| Library | Version | Source | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| bzip2 / libbz2 | 1.0.8 | https://sourceware.org/bzip2/ | BSD-style (see `LICENSE` in the bzip2 source archive) |
| libxml2 | 2.9.14 | https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/libxml2 | MIT (see `Copyright` in the libxml2 source archive) |

If you redistribute the built libraries, include their license texts.
